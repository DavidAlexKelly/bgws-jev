# Bolling (1995): the Joint Theater Level Simulation

**Source:** Robert H. Bolling (Technology Division, Joint Warfighting
Center, Fort Monroe), "The Joint Theater Level Simulation in Military
Operations Other Than War", August 1995. DTIC ADA328714.

**File:** `raw/simulation/bolling-1995-jtls-in-operations-other-than-war.pdf`
(6 pages). Read on 22 November 2026.

## What it is

A short description of JTLS (version 1.85B) and its use for peace
operations. It describes another simulation's design, not measured figures.

## What it says (PDF p. 1–2)

- **Scale:** theatre level. Ground units are aggregated at battalion or
  brigade size; individual aircraft and ships are modelled.
- **Attrition:** ground combat is Lanchestrian. Individual weapons fired
  from specific platforms (air munitions, artillery, SAMs) are stochastic.
- **Map:** hexes over a Lambert conformal projection; a play box of
  2,000 × 2,000 nautical miles. Beyond that the projection distorts too
  much.
- **Sides:** from two to ten. Relationships between sides can be
  asymmetric and can change during play.
- **Intelligence:** each side builds its own picture of the others from
  its collection assets (recce, HUMINT, SOF, sensors) and may share it.
- **Factions:** each side has factions, which can form, split and change
  sides during play.

## Limits

No performance or calibration figures. Useful only as a design comparison.
