## What it is

Radiation from natural and man-made sources: blackbody theory,
laboratory sources, targets, and natural backgrounds (sky, terrain,
sea). We wanted a vehicle's thermal contrast, for the camouflage model's
ΔT (`world/sensing.CAMO_DT_K`).

## What it says on vehicles (§2.4.1, pp. 108–110 [120–122])

- "Specific radiation characteristics are seldom, if ever, reported in
  the open literature." Vehicle signatures are computed from faceted
  models (Figs. 2.57–2.60: a 6×6 shop van, an APC, a Bradley).
- Painted surfaces have an infrared emissivity of **about 0.9**, changed
  by weathering, dust and dirt. Exhausts are the hottest parts, and
  suppressors on military vehicles make them less certain.

## Use

**Nothing applied.** There is no vehicle ΔT to source `CAMO_DT_K` (2 K,
O'Kane's worked example). Vol. 5 gives 1.5 K as a design contrast
for a vehicle in the open. Camouflage and signature suppression are in
**vol. 7** (*Countermeasure Systems*, ch. 2, Schmieder and Walker), which
is not in the collection. It is now listed as a lead.
