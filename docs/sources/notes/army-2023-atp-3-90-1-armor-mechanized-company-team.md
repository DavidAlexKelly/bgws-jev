# ATP 3-90.1 (2023): Armor and Mechanized Infantry Company Team

**Source:** Headquarters, Department of the Army, ATP 3-90.1, *Armor and
Mechanized Infantry Company Team*, 24 October 2023, incorporating
Change 1 of August 2025. It supersedes the 27 January 2016 edition.
Approved for public release; distribution unlimited.

**File:** `raw/doctrine/army-2023-atp-3-90-1-armor-mechanized-company-team.pdf`
(332 PDF pages; text layer). Read on 23 November 2026 by searching it for
every distance, interval, ratio and percentage, and then reading those
passages. Page references are to the manual's chapter-page numbers.

## What it is

The Army's manual for the tank and mechanised infantry company team:
offence, defence, movement, fire control and sustainment. Like current
doctrine generally, it gives **almost no numbers**. Frontages, intervals
and support-by-fire distances are left to METT-TC (I).

## Figures

| Figure | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Targets hard to tell apart beyond | **2,000 m** "under battlefield conditions" | ¶5-56, p. 5-16 |
| Observed fire is used at engagement ranges | more than 2,500 m | ¶5-64, p. 5-17 |
| TOW best employed at | 2,500–3,700 m, "where targets can be tracked for at least 12 seconds" | ¶3-30, p. 3-5 |
| Mortar final protective fires | normally 100–400 m from friendly troops | ¶8-32, p. 8-8 |
| Company trains | 500–1,000 m (or one terrain feature) behind | ¶3-41, ¶3-142 |
| MICLIC | fired 62 m from the obstacle, clears 100 m deep | ¶C-60 |

**Movement** (¶2-34 to 2-35, p. 2-14):

- Bounding overwatch is "the most secure, but slowest" technique.
- "Bounds should only ever be between covered positions." Without cover
  the company uses traveling overwatch instead, and it "should not
  conduct bounding overwatch across open terrain … while mounted".
- The bounding element "must never move beyond the range at which the
  overwatch element can effectively suppress likely or suspected enemy
  positions". **No distance is given.**

**Fire control** (¶5-63, alternating fire): pairs of elements, such as
two platoons, engage the same target in turn. This holds suppression
longer than volley fire.

## Applied

- **`detection.IDENTIFY_M` (2,000 m)** now cites ¶5-56. The value is
  unchanged.

## Not found (for the leads)

- **No company frontage**, no platoon interval, and no support-by-fire
  standoff distance. The leads' 300 m (FM 3-21.8) and 100 m (FM 17-15)
  intervals are from older manuals, which are not in this collection.
- No force-ratio table.
- No numeric observation sector widths (for `search_fov_deg`). Sectors
  are assigned but not sized.

## Limits

Doctrine says what a company should do, not what happens. The TOW and
2,000 m figures are rules of thumb, not measurements.
