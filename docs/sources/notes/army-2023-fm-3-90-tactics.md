# FM 3-90 (2023): Tactics

**Source:** Headquarters, Department of the Army, FM 3-90, *Tactics*,
1 May 2023. It supersedes FM 3-90-1 and FM 3-90-2 (22 March 2013).
Approved for public release; distribution unlimited.

**File:** `raw/doctrine/army-2023-fm-3-90-tactics.pdf` (480 PDF pages;
text layer). Read on 23 November 2026 by searching for distances,
intervals, ratios and percentages and reading those passages, and the
sections on movement techniques, support by fire and tactical road
marches.

## What it is

The Army's capstone manual on offensive, defensive and enabling tactics,
for brigade and below. Like ATP 3-90.1 it gives few numbers.

## Figures

**Support by fire** (¶A-58, p. A-18; ¶B-60, p. B-12):

- Support-by-fire positions are "within the maximum friendly direct fire
  range of the enemy positions".
- They are "normally located on the flank of an assault force, elevated
  above the objective if possible", so the assault does not mask the
  fire.
- Armoured forces occupy them **hull-down**. Each soldier or weapon gets
  an observation sector, and thermal sights are used to find vehicles
  in tree lines.

**Bounding overwatch** (¶2-20 to 2-22, p. 2-11): one element always halts
to overwatch the other. "Leaders base the length of a bound on the
terrain and the range of overwatching weapons." No distance is given.

**Road march** (¶14-27 to 14-28, p. 14-6):

| | Open column | Close column |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle interval | 50–100 m | 20–25 m |
| Vehicles per km | 15–20 | 40–50 |
| Dismounted soldiers apart | 2–5 m | 1–3 m |

An infantry company in open column occupies "roughly a kilometer of road
or trail".

**Formations** (¶2-15): the box formation can put "up to 50 percent of
the force" in decisive engagement at once.

## Use

- **No figure is changed.** The support-by-fire rules are qualitative:
  flank, elevated, hull-down, within direct-fire range. They describe
  what the attack heuristic already does: support by fire from hull-down
  positions within weapon range.
- The road-march intervals could set convoy spacing, but the land engine
  moves units as single counters.

## Not found (for the leads)

- The lead's quote ("two platoons may suppress while one platoon seizes a
  portion of the company objective") is **not** in this edition. It came
  from an older FM 3-90.
- No company frontage, no support-by-fire standoff, and no force-ratio
  planning table.
